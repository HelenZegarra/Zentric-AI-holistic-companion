# Gentle Exercises for Managing Blood Sugar

### 1. Post-Meal Walk
A 15-minute walk after a meal can significantly lower blood sugar levels. It doesn't need to be strenuous; a gentle stroll is enough to be effective. This helps your muscles use glucose for energy.

### 2. Chair Yoga for Circulation
If you have mobility limitations, chair yoga can improve circulation and flexibility. Simple poses like seated cat-cow or gentle neck rolls are a great starting point.

### 3. Strength Training with Resistance Bands
Using resistance bands for light strength training (like bicep curls or shoulder presses) 2-3 times per week can improve insulin sensitivity. This is a low-impact way to build muscle.
