# Simple Recipes for Type 2 Diabetes

### 1. Quinoa Salad with Roasted Vegetables
This recipe is rich in fiber and complex carbs, which helps manage blood sugar.

**Ingredients:**
* 1 cup cooked quinoa
* 1 cup roasted mixed vegetables (broccoli, bell peppers, zucchini)
* 1/4 cup chickpeas
* 2 tablespoons lemon-tahini dressing

**Instructions:**
1. Combine cooked quinoa, roasted vegetables, and chickpeas in a bowl.
2. Drizzle with lemon-tahini dressing and mix well.
3. Serve warm or cold.

### 2. Chicken and Avocado Lettuce Wraps
A low-carb, high-protein option that's quick and refreshing.

**Ingredients:**
* 1/2 cup shredded cooked chicken breast
* 1/4 cup diced avocado
* 1 tablespoon Greek yogurt or mayo
* 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro
* Large butter lettuce leaves

**Instructions:**
1. In a small bowl, mix shredded chicken, avocado, yogurt, and cilantro.
2. Spoon the mixture into lettuce leaves.
3. Serve immediately.
