# Type 2 Diabetes Diet Guidelines - Blood Sugar Management

Managing Type 2 Diabetes through diet focuses on controlling blood glucose, weight, and cardiovascular health.

**Core Principles:**
* **Carbohydrate Management:** Focus on the *type* and *amount* of carbohydrates. Choose complex carbohydrates with fiber over simple sugars.
* **The Plate Method:** A simple visual guide: Half your plate non-starchy vegetables, one quarter lean protein, one quarter healthy carbohydrates.
* **Consistent Meal Times:** Helps prevent drastic blood sugar swings.
* **Portion Control:** Essential for managing calorie and carbohydrate intake.
* **Healthy Fats:** Prioritize monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats for heart health.
* **Reduce Sodium:** Important for blood pressure management, which is often a concern with Type 2 Diabetes.

**Recommended Foods:**
* **Vegetables:** Abundant non-starchy vegetables (spinach, kale, carrots, tomatoes, broccoli, cauliflower).
* **Fruits:** Whole fruits (apples, berries, citrus) are better than juice due to fiber.
* **Whole Grains:** Brown rice, quinoa, oats, whole-wheat bread and pasta.
* **Lean Protein:** Chicken, turkey, fish, beans, lentils, tofu.
* **Healthy Fats:** Avocados, nuts, seeds, olive oil, fatty fish (salmon).

**Foods to Avoid/Limit:**
* Sugary drinks (soda, sweetened tea, energy drinks).
* Refined grains (white bread, white rice, regular pasta).
* Processed foods, fast food, sugary snacks, and desserts.
* Foods high in saturated and trans fats (butter, bacon, processed meats, fried foods).
* Excessive sodium.
