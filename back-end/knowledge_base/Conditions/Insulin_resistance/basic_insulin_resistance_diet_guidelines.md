# Insulin Resistance Diet Guidelines - Stabilizing Blood Sugar

Dietary strategies for insulin resistance aim to stabilize blood glucose levels, reduce the demand for insulin, and improve the body's response to insulin.

**Key Principles:**
* **Focus on Whole, Unprocessed Foods:** This is the cornerstone.
* **Balanced Macronutrients:** Ensure meals contain a good balance of complex carbohydrates, lean protein, and healthy fats.
* **High Fiber Intake:** Fiber slows down glucose absorption. Excellent sources include vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and legumes.
* **Lean Protein Sources:** Poultry, fish, tofu, beans, and lentils help with satiety and have minimal impact on blood sugar.
* **Healthy Fats:** Monounsaturated (olive oil, avocados) and polyunsaturated fats (nuts, seeds, fatty fish) can improve insulin sensitivity.
* **Portion Control:** Even healthy foods can impact blood sugar if eaten in excessive amounts.
* **Hydration:** Drink plenty of water.

**Foods to Emphasize:**
* Non-starchy vegetables (leafy greens, broccoli, bell peppers)
* Whole grains (quinoa, oats, brown rice, whole-wheat bread)
* Legumes (beans, lentils, chickpeas)
* Berries and other low-GI fruits
* Lean meats and fish
* Nuts and seeds

**Foods to Limit/Avoid:**
* Highly processed foods
* Foods high in added sugars (sugary drinks, candy, desserts)
* Refined carbohydrates (white bread, white rice, regular pasta)
* Excessive saturated and trans fats (fried foods, fast food)
