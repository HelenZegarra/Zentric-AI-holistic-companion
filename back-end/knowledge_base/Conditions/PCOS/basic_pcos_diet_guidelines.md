# PCOS Diet Guidelines - Managing Insulin and Inflammation
For Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), dietary changes focus on improving insulin sensitivity and reducing inflammation.

**Key Recommendations:**
* **Prioritize Low-GI Carbs:** Opt for whole grains (quinoa, brown rice, oats), legumes, and non-starchy vegetables. Avoid refined carbs like white bread, pasta, and sugary drinks.
* **Lean Proteins:** Include chicken, turkey, fish, tofu, and legumes. Protein helps with satiety and blood sugar control.
* **Healthy Fats:** Incorporate avocados, nuts, seeds, and olive oil. Omega-3 rich fish (salmon, mackerel) are beneficial for inflammation.
* **Fiber-Rich Foods:** Vegetables, fruits (especially berries, apples, pears), and whole grains help slow sugar absorption and promote satiety.
* **Dairy:** Some women with PCOS find reducing dairy helpful due to potential inflammatory effects, but this is individual. Low-fat dairy is generally preferred.
* **Limit Processed Foods & Sugar:** These can worsen insulin resistance and inflammation.
* **Regular Meals:** Eating consistent meals and snacks can help stabilize blood sugar.

**Foods to Limit/Avoid:**
* Sugary beverages (soda, fruit juice)
* Refined grains (white bread, pasta)
* Processed snacks and baked goods
* High intake of saturated and trans fats (fried foods, fatty meats)
